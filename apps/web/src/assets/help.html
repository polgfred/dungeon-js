Dungeon of Doom is a small, turn-based dungeon crawler. You explore a 7-floor maze, fight monsters, collect treasures, and decide when to press on or retreat. Your goal is to ascend to the top floor of the dungeon with all ten treasures, and find the exit.

What to Expect

  • Rooms may contain treasure, monsters, vendors, or magical objects.
  • Monsters trigger an encounter where you can fight, run, or cast spells.
  • Scrolls grant spell charges. Spells can protect you, deal damage, weaken foes, or teleport you.
  • Vendors sell better gear, scrolls, and potions.
  • Flares reveal nearby rooms on your current floor.

Interaction

  • N S E W move
  • Arrow keys also move
  • U stairs up (only in a stairs-up room)
  • D stairs down (only in a stairs-down room)
  • X exit (only in the exit room)
  • F flare (reveals nearby rooms)
  • L look in mirror
  • O open chest
  • R read scroll
  • P drink potion
  • B buy from vendor
  • ? help
  • Esc cancel when prompted

Encounter-only

  • F fight
  • R run
  • S cast spell (choose from the spell list)

Vendor prompts

  • W weapons
  • A armour
  • S scrolls
  • P potions
  • Esc cancel/back

Quick actions

  • Shift+S save game
  • Shift+Q quit

Monsters

Monsters increase in difficulty as you progress toward the exit of the dungeon.

  • Skeleton (easiest)
  • Goblin
  • Kobold
  • Orc
  • Troll
  • Werewolf
  • Banshee
  • Hellhound
  • Chimaera
  • Dragon (hardest)
